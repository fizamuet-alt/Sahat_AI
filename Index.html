🤖 Learn & AI Guide

Eye care

  • Take regular breaks during screen use.
  • Read in comfortable light.
  • Do not rub irritated eyes.
  • Tell an adult about blurred vision or eye discomfort.

Ear care

  • Keep listening volume comfortable.
  • Take breaks from headphones.
  • Never put objects into the ear canal.
  • Tell an adult about ear pain or hearing difficulty.
